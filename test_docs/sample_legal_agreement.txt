EXHIBIT A — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

A.1 Uptime Guarantee. Licensor guarantees 99.9% uptime for the Licensed Software during each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows as described in Section A.3.

A.2 Response Times. Licensor shall respond to support tickets based on the following severity levels:
- Critical (system down): Response within 1 hour, resolution within 4 hours
- High (major feature impaired): Response within 4 hours, resolution within 24 hours  
- Medium (minor feature impaired): Response within 8 hours, resolution within 72 hours
- Low (general inquiry): Response within 24 hours

A.3 Scheduled Maintenance. Licensor shall provide at least 48 hours' advance notice of scheduled maintenance. Maintenance windows shall not exceed 4 hours per occurrence and shall be scheduled during off-peak hours (10:00 PM - 6:00 AM EST).

A.4 SLA Credits. If Licensor fails to meet the uptime guarantee in any calendar month, Licensee shall receive service credits as follows: 99.0-99.9% uptime: 5% credit; 95.0-99.0%: 10% credit; below 95.0%: 20% credit. Credits shall be applied against future license fees as provided in Section 4.1.
